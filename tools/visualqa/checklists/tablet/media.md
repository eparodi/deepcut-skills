Image scaling: images must fit their container — no overflow past the frame edge, no stretching or squashing. Evidence in frame: an image crossing the viewport edge or whose shape contradicts its subject is a FAIL.
Aspect ratios: media must not be wrongly cropped, letterboxed, or pillarboxed. Evidence in frame: visible bars, cut-off subjects, or extreme crops are a FAIL.
Video & embeds: a visible player must show play controls and must not cover other content; galleries and thumbnails must be usable by touch. Evidence in frame: a control-less or overlapping player is a FAIL.
Lazy loading: media placeholders must align with the content that loads into them. Evidence in frame: misaligned or visibly jumping content is a FAIL; no visible mismatch is UNCERTAIN from a single frame.

Content containment: at 768px width, long URLs, hashes, JSON, code, or file paths inside a card must wrap or scroll inside their own box, and the page must not scroll horizontally. Evidence in frame: a card or line of text crossing the right viewport edge, a visible horizontal scrollbar, or a page wider than the frame is a FAIL. A horizontally scrollable code/result box is acceptable only when its scroll affordance is visible and the box stays inside the page margin.
App-scale hierarchy: the first screen has one clear h1 and a readable body, and an app screen's title is not a landing-page hero. Evidence in frame: compare the h1 to body text — a title more than about 2.5× the body size, a headline over two lines, or a hero-scale block on a settings/console screen is a FAIL; exact pixel size is UNCERTAIN from a resized frame.
Flat surfaces: panels are separated by a border or a tone step, and shadows are reserved for overlays (menus, dialogs). Evidence in frame: a static card whose shadow makes it read as floating above the ground is a FAIL; a subtle card shadow that also has a 1px border is UNCERTAIN, not a FAIL.
Meaningful motion and figures: any motion signals state or loading, no decorative infinite motion competes with content, loading feedback is visible, and numeric figures use stable alignment. Evidence in frame: a loading state with no visible indicator, or an infinite decorative animation crossing the content, is a FAIL; animation quality, reduced-motion behavior, and exact figure alignment are UNCERTAIN from a static frame.

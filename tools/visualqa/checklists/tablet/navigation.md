Nav fit: navigation must suit the tablet form factor — a sidebar or top nav works; a hamburger is acceptable but must be visible. Evidence in frame: a screen with no visible navigation control is a FAIL.
Back affordance: deeper pages must show a back control or breadcrumbs. Evidence in frame: none visible in the frame is a FAIL.
Drawers & menus: an open drawer must show a close affordance (scrim, X, or close label) and must not permanently cover content. Evidence in frame: check the visible overlay for a clear dismissal.
Location cue: the current section must be identifiable — active nav state, breadcrumb, or page title. Evidence in frame: inability to tell where you are is a FAIL.
